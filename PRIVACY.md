# VaultPony — Privacy Policy

VaultPony does not collect, transmit, or share any data. There is no
account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, and no
network access of any kind — VaultPony has no server to talk to and makes
no outbound network requests.

Everything VaultPony creates or touches — vault files, recent-vaults list,
app settings, any biometric-unlock credential — stays on your device. A
biometric-unlock credential, if you turn that feature on for a vault, is
stored in the device's Keychain, protected by Face ID/Touch ID, and never
leaves the device.

VaultPony is open source. You can read exactly what the app does at
<https://github.com/norsehorse-dev/VaultPonyIOS>.

registered trademark of IDRIX.

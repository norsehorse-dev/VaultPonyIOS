VaultPony creates and opens encrypted containers on iPhone and iPad. A vault is a single encrypted file that holds a private filesystem, and only your password opens it. The containers are compatible with VeraCrypt, so you can open the same vaults on the desktop and back.

There is no account, no cloud, and no network access. Your vaults are just files, kept wherever you choose — On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, or a third-party storage provider.

Features:

Create and open VeraCrypt-compatible containers, with a choice of cipher, hash, and filesystem (FAT or exFAT).

Hidden volumes: conceal a second vault inside the first. Two passwords, and the outer one gives no sign the hidden one exists. The outer volume can be write-protected so normal use will not overwrite the hidden data.

Header backup and recovery, in-place password change, and keyfiles.

Face ID unlock, auto-lock when the app leaves the foreground, a privacy screen that hides vault content in the app switcher, and a no-trace mode that never writes decrypted content outside the vault.

A full split-view layout on iPad — browse and preview side by side.

Localized in English, German, Spanish, French, Russian, and Brazilian Portuguese.
